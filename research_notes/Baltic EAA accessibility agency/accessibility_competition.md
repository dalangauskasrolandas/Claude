# Competitive Landscape: Web Accessibility (EAA / WCAG) Services in Lithuania, Latvia and Estonia

> Method note (read first): research date 30 Oct 2026. All findings come from web-search result summaries of the cited pages. Direct page fetching was blocked in this session for some hosts (redwall.ee and twn.ee failed to resolve), so I fetched no pages directly. The per-session web-search budget also ran out before the final planned searches (German monitoring prices, more Latvian/Estonian agencies, LinkedIn counts). Prices below are as reported in search snippets of the provider pages. **Check each one on the live page before using it in client-facing material.** Where a snippet showed a page's index age, I flag it. Anything marked **[Judgment]** or **[Estimate]** is mine, not from a source.

## 1. Lithuania: who sells EAA/WCAG audits and remediation, and at what price?

### Takeaway
Lithuania has a visible but fragmented supply. There is one dedicated specialist (AxAll, partnered with LASS, quote-only) and a long tail of web, SEO and UX agencies that published EAA explainer content in 2025–2026. A few of them post price ranges, from about €150–400 for a basic audit up to €5,000+ for full optimisation. I found no one selling a productised "scan → audit + fixes + statement → monthly monitoring" SME package with transparent monthly pricing.

### Cited Findings
**Specialists**
- **AxAll (axall.digital).** A dedicated accessibility consultancy. It publishes no EAA audit price: every engagement is scoped individually, and cost "depends on scope, complexity, and whether you need full ACR documentation". It says it works "with accessibility specialists at the Lithuanian Union of the Blind and Partially Sighted (LASS) on every relevant evaluation". Its site also argues that overlays do not replace code fixes. — [AxAll](https://www.axall.digital/)
- AxAll says its co-founder and lead accessibility specialist was the first person in Lithuania to earn the IAAP WAS certification. It also says a new UX/accessibility engineer (Lina Balčiūnaitė) holds IAAP CPACC. — [AxAll insights: Meet Lina](https://www.axall.digital/insights/growing-stronger-in-accessibility-meet-lina)
- **June / Karlove Agency + LASS.** Kaunas University of Technology (KTU) published accessibility conformance reports based on audits by "June/Karlove Agency" and LASS. The sample was 21 pages on the main site and 5 on the student site. Two testers did the work, one blind and one sighted. Price: not published. — [KTU prieinamumo atitikties ataskaita](https://ktu.edu/prieinamumo-atitikties-ataskaita/)

**Agencies with published prices or price ranges**
- **BMK (portfolio.bmk.lt).** Published packages (EN page):
  - Basic audit from €900: an automatic WCAG 2.1 AA check.
  - Audit + expert assessment from €2,500.
  - Full optimisation (automatic + manual WCAG audit, with fixes) from €5,000.
  — [BMK Services](https://portfolio.bmk.lt/en/services/)
- **web1o (web1o.lt).** Blog estimates:
  - Audit + priority plan: about €150–400.
  - Quick fixes: about €300–800.
  - More complex work: about €500–1,500.
  - Typical total: about €800–2,500 one-off, "plus small ongoing maintenance".
  It also names VVTAT as the supervisor. — [web1o: Svetainės prieinamumas](https://web1o.lt/lt/tinklarastis/svetaines-prieinamumas-neigaliesiems)
- **uxauditas.lt.** Sells UX audits that cover WCAG principles: usually €400–800 + VAT, from €300 for simple sites, and over €1,000 for large platforms. This is a UX audit, not a dedicated conformance audit. — [UX auditas](https://uxauditas.lt/)

**Agencies offering accessibility audits, prices not published in the snippets I saw**
- **Dizi (dizi.lt).** "Svetainių prieinamumo (WCAG) vertinimas ir auditas". Offers two levels: a simplified assessment for periodic control, and a detailed one for compliance reporting or new systems. The report rates each criterion as meets / partially meets / does not meet, with locations and fix recommendations, and a re-test after fixes. Price: not published (not shown in search). — [Dizi testavimai](https://dizi.lt/musu-teikiamos-paslaugos/testavimai/)
- **Tobalt (tobalt.lt).** Checks WCAG 2.1 AA conformance and real barriers in navigation and forms, and can review cookie/GDPR touchpoints. It advertises a **free accessibility report** as a lead magnet. Price: not published. — [Tobalt technologijų auditas](https://tobalt.lt/it-patarimai/technologiju-auditas/); [Tobalt Prieinamumas (WCAG)](https://tobalt.lt/prieinamumas-wcag/); [Tobalt EN accessibility](https://tobalt.eu/accessibility/)
- **Rebi (rebi.lt).** Offers a **free WCAG check tool** that scans HTML for alt texts, form labels and headings, described as a first pass rather than a full audit. — [Rebi svetainės auditas](https://rebi.lt/svetaines-auditas)
- **webaudit.lt.** Advertises a "Nemokamas svetainės auditas" (free website audit). The snippet did not show whether accessibility is covered. — [webaudit.lt](https://webaudit.lt/)

**Legal and market context used by LT sellers**
- EAA obligations apply from 28 June 2025 under the Lithuanian Law on Accessibility of Products and Services. They cover websites, apps, e-commerce and similar services. VVTAT (State Consumer Rights Protection Authority) supervises. Micro-enterprises (<10 staff and ≤€2m turnover or balance sheet) are exempt. — [VVTAT: Prieinamumo direktyva](https://vvtat.lrv.lt/lt/prieinamumo-reikalavimai/bendra-informacija/); [VVTAT primena](https://vvtat.lrv.lt/lt/naujienos-ir-pranesimai-ziniasklaidai-377/vvtat-primena-apie-prieinamumo-reikalavimu-isigaliojima/)
- Fines are reported as €500–15,000 by BNS/15min (2025) — [15min / BNS](https://www.15min.lt/verslas/naujiena/finansai/prieinamumo-direktyva-isigalios-jau-birzeli-imoniu-laukia-naujos-pareigos-ir-baudos-662-2462208). A third-party blog reports €10,000–100,000 instead, which conflicts — [Clym](https://www.clym.io/blog/european-accessibility-act-fines-by-country). The BNS figure comes from a local news agency and is more credible.

### Inferences
- **[Judgment]** Supply splits into three tiers:
  1. A credentialed specialist (AxAll, WAS/CPACC, LASS partnership) aimed at larger clients and ACR-grade work.
  2. Mid-market agencies (BMK, Dizi, Tobalt) that sell audits at €900–5,000+.
  3. Small web shops whose blog content anchors buyers' expectations at €150–400 for an audit and under €2,500 all-in (web1o).
- **[Judgment]** The client's €-priced "free scan → audit + fixes + statement" funnel would compete directly with tier 3 on price and with Tobalt and Rebi on the "free scan" hook. Monthly monitoring looks like the least contested element in Lithuania, because I found no provider advertising it.

### Gaps
- Prices for AxAll, Dizi, Tobalt and the June/Karlove agency are not published, or the snippets did not show them.
- One search summary described a Lithuanian agency offering an audit from about €300, audit + fixes from about €600 and a full package from about €850. The search tool did not attribute it to a URL, so it is **unverified** and excluded from the price findings.
- I could not verify the size of any LT provider (headcount, revenue). Lursoft/Rekvizitai/LinkedIn lookups were not possible.

## 2. Latvia: who sells EAA/WCAG audits and remediation, and at what price?

### Takeaway
Latvia has a small number of identifiable providers: pieklustamiba.lv, pieejams.lv, Cube (Riga) and SEM audits, plus Skrivanek for plain-language text audits. Only pieklustamiba.lv publishes prices, and its page may be about two years old: audit €1,500, statement €300, bundle €1,650.

### Cited Findings
- **pieklustamiba.lv.** Published prices:
  - Audit + accessibility statement bundle: €1,650, delivered in about two weeks.
  - Website audit alone: €1,500. Includes a VARAM-format protocol, an explanation of each point, a results presentation and testing with real users.
  - Accessibility statement alone: €300, including help publishing it.
  It also offers a 2-day practical accessibility training. **Older information flag:** the search index showed the price page as indexed about 743 days earlier (around late 2024). — [Pieklustamiba.lv Pakalpojumi](https://www.pieklustamiba.lv/pakalpojumi/); [Piekļūstamības nodrošināšana](https://www.pieklustamiba.lv/pieklustamibas-nodrosinasana/)
- pieklustamiba.lv also published a study finding accessibility gaps on **all** of Latvia's TOP 100 most influential companies' websites. Its related package for those companies costs €1,360–2,520 excl. VAT. — [Pieklustamiba.lv TOP 100 pētījums](https://www.pieklustamiba.lv/petijums-visas-top-100-latvijas-ietekmigako-uznemumu-timekla-vietnes-ir-nepilnibas-digitalas-vides-pieklustamiba/)
- **pieejams.lv.** Audits against WCAG 2.1 AA and EN 301 549, combining automated tools (axe, WAVE, Lighthouse) with manual keyboard and screen-reader checks. A standard audit takes about 2 weeks, and it also prepares the accessibility statement. Price: **not published.** The site offers a free consultation and a quote on request, and its structured data shows only "€€". — [pieejams.lv](https://pieejams.lv/); [pieejams.lv contact](https://pieejams.lv/contact)
- **Cube / CUBE Aģentūra SIA (Riga, reg. no. 44103066717).** A digital agency positioning itself as an "Accessibility Assurance Company". It sells EN 301 549 audits (ACT methodology: automated + manual expert testing) that produce a compliance score, a full defect inventory and prioritised fixes. It also implements the fixes and claims "internationally certified accessibility experts". Price: **not published**; it depends on the number of views and interaction complexity. Cube's site says the supervisor can fine up to €10,000, repeatable until fixed (vendor claim). — [Cube accessibility audit](https://www.cube.lv/en/services/accessibility-audit); [Cube implementation](https://www.cube.lv/en/pakalpojums/implementation-of-accessibility); [Lursoft](https://company.lursoft.lv/en/cube-agentura/44103066717)
- **SEM (audits.sem.lv).** Website audit and testing service that includes WCAG-based accessibility testing with manual checks. Price: not published (not shown in search). — [audits.sem.lv](https://audits.sem.lv/)
- **Skrivanek Latvia.** "Teksta saprotamības audits" (text comprehensibility audit) aligned to the Accessibility Directive. This is a niche, content-side complement rather than a code audit. — [Skrivanek](https://skrivanek.lv/teksta-saprotamibas-audits/)
- **Cobalt (law firm).** Offers a short general readiness check of companies against the Latvian Law on Accessibility of Goods and Services (PPPL). — [Cobalt](https://www.cobalt.legal/lv/news-cases/precu-un-pakalpojumu-pieklustamiba-vai-uznemumi-ir-gatavi-es-direktivas-izpildei/)
- **mansjumis.lv.** Publishes a guide on the digital accessibility law, requirements and practical implementation in Latvia. It is likely agency or hosting content marketing; its offer was not verified. — [mansjumis.lv](https://mansjumis.lv/blogs/digitala-pieejamiba-latvija)

### Inferences
- **[Judgment]** Latvia's published price anchor (€1,500 audit / €1,650 with statement) is about 2× Lithuania's low-end anchors. That leaves room for a lower-priced productised SME offer, although the figure may be dated.
- **[Judgment]** Fewer providers were found than in Lithuania or Estonia, and only one publishes prices. Supply looks thinner, and also less visible in search.

### Gaps
- I found no Latvian provider advertising monthly monitoring or a subscription.
- Current (2026) pieklustamiba.lv prices are not confirmed.
- No Russian-language Latvian providers were found. I ran no RU-language search before the budget ran out.

## 3. Estonia: who sells EAA/WCAG audits and remediation, and at what price?

### Takeaway
Estonia has a mature UX/accessibility consulting scene, led by Trinidad Wiseman (TWN), which also helped the state regulator TTJA with its 2024 monitoring. Redwall, Ligipääsuke and the Estonian Blind Union also offer testing. Most commercial prices are unpublished. The one hard anchor is the Estonian Blind Union's **€45/hour**, with typical audits of 8–30 hours.

### Cited Findings
- **Trinidad Wiseman (twn.ee).** Maps accessibility problems in web environments, apps and documents such as PDFs against EN 301 549 and WCAG 2.2 AA. Also offers user testing and training: for example, a 3-hour accessibility training for Coop bank, and sessions for the University of Tartu. Audit and training prices: **not published.** — [TWN Ligipääsetavus](https://www.twn.ee/teenused/ligipaasetavus); [TWN Academy](https://www.twn.ee/en/trinidad-wiseman-academy)
- TTJA's 2024 public-sector digital accessibility monitoring covered 132 websites and 8 mobile apps, and was carried out with help from Trinidad Wiseman OÜ. — [TTJA 2024 report (PDF)](https://ttja.ee/sites/default/files/documents/2024-12/Aruanne_Eesti%20avaliku%20sektori%20digiligip%C3%A4%C3%A4setavus%202024.%20aastal_31.12.2024.pdf)
- **Redwall (redwall.ee).** Digital marketing and web agency. Its accessibility audit maps WCAG conformance using automatic tools plus an experienced specialist, and lists fixes. Price: **not published.** — [Redwall website accessibility audit](https://www.redwall.ee/en/services/website-audit/website-accessibility-audit/); [Redwall kodulehe audit](https://redwall.ee/teenused/kodulehe-audit/)
- **Ligipääsuke (ligipaasuke.ee).** Digital accessibility testing and consulting at two levels:
  - Full audit for complete WCAG 2.2 / EN 301 549 assurance.
  - Lighter test of the most critical elements, for early phases or small budgets.
  Price: **not published**; it depends on size. — [Ligipääsuke teenused](https://ligipaasuke.ee/teenused/digiligipaasetavuse-testimine-ja-noustamine/)
- **Eesti Pimedate Liit (Estonian Blind Union).** Web and mobile accessibility testing and consulting at **€45/hour**. A typical audit takes **8–30 hours**, depending on pages or app size, and the initial consultation is free. — [Pimedate Liit testimine ja nõustamine](https://pimedateliit.ee/testimine-ja-noustamine/); [Pimedate Liit veebi ligipääsetavus](https://pimedateliit.ee/veebi-ligipaasetavus/)
  - **[Estimate]** At €45/h, 8–30 h is about €360–1,350 per audit, before any VAT.
- **Digitugi (digitugi.eu).** Accessibility audit for e-learning environments: **€900–1,500.** — [Digitugi tark e-õpe](https://digitugi.eu/tark-e-ope/)
- **Websystems (websystems.ee).** Web agency publishing EAA content ("Ligipääsetavus 2025 – kohustus või võimalus") and its own WCAG 2.2 AA / EN 301 549 declaration. Paid offer and price not shown. — [Websystems article](https://www.websystems.ee/kasulikku-lugemist/ligipaasetavus-2025-kohustus-voi-voimalus/); [Websystems declaration](https://www.websystems.ee/ligipaasetavus/)
- **Legal context.**
  - Requirements apply to e-shops from 28 June 2025.
  - Service micro-enterprises are exempt (<10 staff, turnover and balance sheet ≤€2m).
  - TTJA supervises and checks on its own initiative. — [E-kaubanduse Liit kiirtest](https://www.e-kaubanduseliit.ee/uudised/ligipaasetavuse-kiirtest-e-poodidele)
  - Law firm Triniti cites fines of up to €20,000. — [Triniti](https://triniti.eu/et/uudised/uued-ligipaasetavusnouded-e-kaubandusele/)

### Inferences
- **[Judgment]** In Estonia the toughest price competitor for an SME audit may be the Estonian Blind Union, a non-profit charging about €360–1,350, rather than an agency. Its user-expert credibility is hard for an offshore-delivered offer to match.
- **[Judgment]** Estonia's strong in-house digital capability and an established UX consultancy (TWN) working with the regulator suggest that buyers are more sophisticated and more sceptical of overlays and automated-only offers.

### Gaps
- Commercial prices from TWN, Redwall and Ligipääsuke are not published.
- I could not fetch provider pages directly (DNS blocked for .ee hosts in this session).
- No Russian-language Estonian providers were researched.

## 4. Web agencies that added EAA/accessibility packages (incl. free or bundled offers)

### Takeaway
In all three countries, many general web, SEO and UX agencies published EAA explainer content in 2025–2026 to capture search demand. Few publish a priced package. "Free" offers are common as lead magnets: free reports or checkers from Tobalt, Rebi and webaudit.lt in LT, and free consultations from pieejams.lv in LV and the Blind Union in EE.

  - Redwall — [Redwall blog](https://redwall.ee/blogi/316-ligipaasetav-veeb-on-parem-koigile/)

### Inferences
- **[Judgment]** Content saturation is high relative to priced, productised offers. Many agencies wrote one EAA article in 2025 but appear to sell accessibility as an add-on to web builds or redesigns, not as a standalone subscription. That leaves a gap for a focused, priced SME package. It also means local agencies are likely to treat the client as either a white-label supplier or a competitor.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm which of these agencies actually sell a priced EAA package versus only publishing content. Their price pages were not visible in search.
- I found no agency explicitly bundling accessibility "free" into new website builds, though this may exist.

## 5. Overlay/widget vendors and resellers active in the Baltics

### Takeaway
Global overlays (accessiBe, UserWay, EqualWeb, Equally AI) cost about $39–$479 per month. They are visible in the Baltics mainly through individual company declarations, for example a Lithuanian retailer citing EqualWeb, rather than through local reseller programmes. The leading local specialist, AxAll, explicitly argues against overlays.

### Cited Findings
- **accessiBe.** Monthly tiers of $59 (Micro), $179 (Growth) and $479 (Scale), or $490 / $1,490 / $3,990 billed annually, keyed to traffic. Enterprise pricing above 100k monthly visits is custom. — [PricingSaaS accessiBe](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/accessibe); [TestParty accessiBe pricing](https://testparty.ai/blog/accessibe-pricing). Capterra shows a lower entry of $49/month (usage-based), which conflicts — [Capterra accessiBe](https://www.capterra.com/p/212877/accessiBe/pricing/)
- **UserWay.** Capterra lists Widget Pro at $69/month and Pro Plus at $169/month — [Capterra UserWay](https://www.capterra.com/p/218549/UserWay/pricing/). TestParty puts entry at about $49–69/month and the top published tier at about $249–359/month — [TestParty UserWay pricing](https://testparty.ai/blog/userway-pricing)
- **EqualWeb.** PricingSaaS lists widget tiers of $39, $49, $109 and $140.83 per month, plus a managed tier — [PricingSaaS EqualWeb](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/equalweb). Capterra lists monitoring-scan tiers from $590/year (100 pages) to $9,990/year (100k pages) — [Capterra EqualWeb](https://www.capterra.com/p/233808/Equalweb/reviews/). TechRadar notes a managed plan with in-house expert remediation — [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/reviews/equalweb)
- **Equally AI.** From 45/month (currency not stated), with a free version and trial. — [GetApp Equally AI](https://www.getapp.com/finance-accounting-software/a/equally-ai/)
- **Eye-Able** (German): pricing is quote-based and not published. — [Guideflow](https://www.guideflow.com/blog/digital-accessibility-software)
- **Baltic usage evidence:** a search of Lithuanian corporate accessibility declarations surfaced one retailer stating "Bendrovė Svetainėje įdiegė automatizuotą prieinamumo sprendimą, kurį teikia platforma „EqualWeb“". Pages in that result set included audimas.lt, wisetg.com, hohesc.lt and bonduelle.lt; the search tool did not say which one this was. — [Audimas declaration](https://www.audimas.lt/informacija-klientui/prieinamumo-deklaracija/); [Wise TG declaration](https://wisetg.com/lt/prieinamumo-deklaracija)
- A Lithuanian site mentions a widget called "Kloosive" that auto-fixes some WCAG issues; no price was found (low confidence). A WordPress overlay plugin, OneTap, has Lithuanian-language pages. — [wponetap LT](https://lt.wponetap.com/tegus/%C4%AFtraukus-dizainas/puslapis/3/)
- **AxAll** (LT specialist) states that automated overlay solutions do not replace real code fixes. — [AxAll](https://www.axall.digital/)

### Inferences
- **[Judgment]** Overlays are a price-anchoring threat: an SME can buy a "compliance" widget for about €40–70/month. That is similar to what the client might charge for monitoring, so the client's monitoring offer must be clearly differentiated as human-verified, with fixes and an up-to-date statement.
- **[Judgment]** I found no evidence of formal local overlay resellers or agency partner programmes in the Baltics. This means low channel competition from overlays, but also little local market education against them.

### Gaps
- No Eye-Able, accessiBe or UserWay Baltic partners or resellers were found.
- No count of Baltic sites running overlays was possible. A BuiltWith/Wappalyzer ccTLD analysis would be needed.
- Euro prices for Equally AI and Eye-Able are not confirmed.

## 6. International/Nordic players targeting the Baltics, and Baltic-based specialists / IAAP professionals

### Takeaway
I found no evidence that Siteimprove, Funka, Level Access, Deque, Monsido/Acquia, Silktide or Pope Tech actively target Baltic SMEs: no Baltic offices, partners or customer cases surfaced. The only clearly IAAP-credentialed Baltic team found is AxAll in Lithuania (first WAS in LT, plus CPACC staff). Cube in Latvia claims "internationally certified" experts without naming the credentials.

### Cited Findings
- **Siteimprove.** Partner of W3C, IAAP and the UN, with offices in 15 countries. Its nearest regional link is a Helsinki office opened for Nordic accessibility work; no Baltic office or customer was found. — [Siteimprove Helsinki office](https://www.siteimprove.com/press/siteimprove-to-open-helsinki-office-with-extensive-accessibility-expertise/); [Siteimprove EAA press release](https://www.siteimprove.com/press/siteimprove-customers-are-leading-the-way-ahead-of-the-european-accessibility-act/)
- **Funka** (Sweden). Sells IAAP CPACC certification-preparation training, which is open internationally; no Baltic customers found. — [Funka CPACC training](https://www.funka.com/en/funka-academy/f%C3%B6rberedande-certifiering/cpacc-certification-preparatory-training-courses/)
- **Silktide.** UK G-Cloud pricing of £6,000–39,800 per unit per year. **Older information flag:** from a 2024 pricing document. — [G-Cloud Silktide](https://www.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud-14/services/608269605939939). Silktide also offers a free accessibility scan and Chrome extension — [Silktide free scan](https://silktide.com/free-accessibility-scan/)
- **AxAll (LT):** first WAS-certified person in Lithuania, plus a CPACC-certified engineer. — [AxAll insights](https://www.axall.digital/insights/growing-stronger-in-accessibility-meet-lina)
- **Cube (LV):** claims "internationally certified accessibility experts" (vendor claim, credentials not named). — [Cube implementation](https://www.cube.lv/en/pakalpojums/implementation-of-accessibility)
- **Trinidad Wiseman (EE):** helped TTJA with its 2024 national monitoring. Individual certifications not verified. — [TTJA 2024 report](https://ttja.ee/sites/default/files/documents/2024-12/Aruanne_Eesti%20avaliku%20sektori%20digiligip%C3%A4%C3%A4setavus%202024.%20aastal_31.12.2024.pdf)

### Inferences

## 10. Per-country saturation assessment [Judgment]

### Takeaway
**[Judgment]**
- **Lithuania: MEDIUM.**
- **Latvia: LOW–MEDIUM.**
- **Estonia: MEDIUM**, with high buyer sophistication and a strong low-cost non-profit competitor.

In all three countries, the monthly monitoring/subscription layer for SMEs appears largely **unoccupied** by local providers, while the "free scan" hook is already used by several competitors and public bodies.

### Cited Findings (evidence underpinning the judgment)
- **LT:**
  - About 8 providers with explicit audit offers, including a credentialed specialist with an NGO partnership — [AxAll](https://www.axall.digital/); [KTU/June/Karlove/LASS](https://ktu.edu/prieinamumo-atitikties-ataskaita/)
  - Low price anchors already published: audit €150–400 — [web1o](https://web1o.lt/lt/tinklarastis/svetaines-prieinamumas-neigaliesiems); audit from €900 — [BMK](https://portfolio.bmk.lt/en/services/)
  - Free scan/report hooks — [Tobalt](https://tobalt.lt/prieinamumas-wcag/); [Rebi](https://rebi.lt/svetaines-auditas)
  - LASS free self-assessment for Charter members in 2026 — [Diversity.lt](https://diversity.lt/programos/internetiniu-svetainiu-prieinamumo-vertinimai/)
- **LV:**
  - About 5 providers; only one with published prices (€1,500 audit / €300 statement, possibly dated) — [pieklustamiba.lv](https://www.pieklustamiba.lv/pakalpojumi/)
  - Strongest 2026 regulator push — [PTAC prioritātes](https://www.ptac.gov.lv/lv/ptac-prioritates); [PTAC campaign](https://www.ptac.gov.lv/lv/ptac-kampana-neredzama-skerslu-josla)
  - Free state tool — [VARAM](https://pieklustamiba.varam.gov.lv/)
- **EE:**
  - Established UX/accessibility consultancy working with the regulator — [TWN](https://www.twn.ee/teenused/ligipaasetavus); [TTJA 2024](https://ttja.ee/sites/default/files/documents/2024-12/Aruanne_Eesti%20avaliku%20sektori%20digiligip%C3%A4%C3%A4setavus%202024.%20aastal_31.12.2024.pdf)
  - Blind Union at €45/h — [Pimedate Liit](https://pimedateliit.ee/testimine-ja-noustamine/)
  - Free e-shop quick test from the e-commerce association — [E-kaubanduse Liit](https://www.e-kaubanduseliit.ee/uudised/ligipaasetavuse-kiirtest-e-poodidele)
  - Multiple agencies with EAA content — [Nobel Digital](https://www.nobeldigital.ee/blogi/kas-sinu-e-pood-vastab-juunis-joustuvatele-ligipaasetavuse-nouetele/); [Opus](https://www.opus.ee/en/journal/accessible-e-commerce-in-2025/)

### Inferences
**[Judgment]**
- **Lithuania (MEDIUM).** Plenty of agencies compete for search attention, and the low-price end is already contested (€150–400 audits, free reports). However, no one appears to sell a transparent SME subscription with monitoring and statement upkeep. Entry is feasible, but differentiation must come from the productised bundle, Lithuanian-language deliverables and a credible validation partner (for example LASS-style user testing), not from price alone.
- **Latvia (LOW–MEDIUM).** Thinnest visible supply and highest published price anchor. PTAC's 2026 enforcement focus makes it the most favourable demand-versus-supply ratio of the three. Risks:
  - Free PTAC and VARAM self-check tools.
  - Potential Russian-language market segments not researched.
  - The client's Lithuania-first sequencing means Latvia's window may narrow as local players (Cube, pieejams.lv) scale.
- **Estonia (MEDIUM).** Fewer obvious competitors by count than Lithuania, but higher quality (TWN, Ligipääsuke). Buyers are digitally sophisticated, and a non-profit sells expert-user testing at about €45/h. An offshore-delivered, lower-touch model may struggle on credibility. Positioning on monitoring and ongoing fixes, rather than one-off audits, is the most open angle.
- **Cross-cutting:**
  - Overlays at about $40–70/month set the price ceiling buyers compare monitoring against.
  - The micro-enterprise exemption shrinks the obliged-SME pool in all three countries.
  - Both factors moderate the size of the opportunity regardless of saturation.

### Gaps
- Saturation is judged from providers visible in search, not a full census.
- Without Google Ads competition data, LinkedIn activity counts or local business-readiness surveys, the demand side rests mainly on regulator activity and provider counts. Check these assessments with keyword-tool data and a few calls to local agencies before committing budget.
